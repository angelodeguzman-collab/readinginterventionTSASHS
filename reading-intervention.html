About the program

Reading is the skill behind every subject. Read to Rise finds where each learner is stuck and gives them regular, focused practice until reading feels natural.

Who it is for

Learners whose assessment shows they read below grade level, and any learner who asks for extra support.

What we aim for

Move every participant up at least one reading level by the end of the school year, and build the habit of reading for fun.

How it works

Learners are assessed, grouped by level, meet their teacher-mentor weekly, and are re-assessed to see how far they have come.

How parents help

Ten minutes of reading together at home each day makes a real difference. See the Resources section for ideas.

Guided reading

A teacher-mentor reads with a small group and coaches pronunciation and pacing.

Partner reading

Learners take turns reading aloud and retelling what they heard.

Word of the day

One new word, used in a sentence, a drawing and a short conversation.

Book talk Fridays

Resources for home

Simple things families can do with any reading material: a textbook, a newspaper, a storybook or a text message thread.

For parents

Ten minutes a day

  • Pick a quiet time, such as after dinner.
  • Let your child choose what to read.
  • Ask, "What happened and why?" instead of "Did you finish?"
  • Praise effort and persistence.
For students

Read it three times

  • First read: get the main idea.
  • Second read: underline words you do not know.
  • Third read: say the passage in your own words.
  • Keep a notebook of new words.

Reading assessments

Learners read a passage aloud and answer comprehension questions. The teacher records accuracy and understanding, then places the learner in one of three levels, as in the Philippine Informal Reading Inventory (Phil-IRI).

Frustration

Comprehension below 59%

The text is too hard to read alone. The learner needs close guidance from a teacher.

Instructional

Comprehension 59–79%

The learner reads well with teacher support. This is the best level for learning.

Independent

Comprehension 80% and above

The learner reads and understands comfortably without help.

Start of year

Pre-test

Sets the starting level and decides which group the learner joins.

Mid-year

Progress check

A short passage to see whether the learner is ready to move to the next group.

End of year

Post-test

Measures growth and shows what to work on in the next school year.

Levels follow the Phil-IRI categories. Confirm the cut-off scores with the school's reading coordinator before publishing.
